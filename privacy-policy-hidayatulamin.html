Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hidayatulamin website ("we", "our", "the Site") collects, uses, and protects your information in connection with Yayasan Hidayatulamin Sagatama's programs and the Pi Network ecosystem.

Summary: We collect only the minimum data needed to operate this site and its social programs. Your Pi identity is used solely for authentication. Application data for scholarships and social assistance is kept confidential and used only for program administration.

Article 1

Information We Collect

When you use the Hidayatulamin website, we may collect:

  • Pi Network identity: Your Pi username and Pi UID, provided by the Pi SDK upon authentication.
  • Program applications: Name, contact details, and supporting information submitted for scholarships or social assistance programs.
  • Firebase (hidayatulamin project): Data stored in Firestore including submitted applications, program participation records, and user preferences.
  • Device & usage data: Browser type, device, pages visited — collected anonymously to improve performance.
Article 2

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity via Pi Network SDK for member features.
  • To process and administer scholarship and social assistance applications.
  • To communicate with applicants regarding program status and updates.
  • To connect your account across the Sagatama ecosystem apps (Sagatama Mart, Ecosystem).
  • To improve the website and its programs.
  • To comply with applicable Indonesian laws and Pi Network platform policies.
Article 3

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (project: hidayatulamin). We apply Firestore security rules to restrict data access to authorized personnel only. All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption. Program application data is accessible only to designated foundation administrators.

Article 4

Third-Party Services

  • Pi Network / Minepi.com — user authentication via Pi SDK.
  • Google Firebase (hidayatulamin) — database, authentication, and hosting.
  • Vercel — app hosting and deployment.

We do not share program application data with any third parties outside those listed above.

Article 5

Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information in your applications or profile.
  • Delete your data by contacting us directly.
  • Withdraw from programs at any time without penalty.
Article 6

Sensitive Program Data

Information submitted for scholarship or social assistance applications (including financial or family situation details) is treated as sensitive data. It is used solely for program evaluation, kept confidential, and not shared outside the foundation's administrative team.

Article 7

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

  • Mematuhi hukum Indonesia dan kebijakan Pi Network.

Penyimpanan dan Keamanan Data

Pasal 4

Layanan Pihak Ketiga

  • Pi Network / Minepi.com — autentikasi pengguna.
  • Google Firebase (hidayatulamin) — database, autentikasi, dan hosting.
  • Vercel — hosting aplikasi.
Pasal 5

Hak-Hak Anda

  • Mengoreksi informasi yang tidak akurat.
  • Menghapus data Anda dengan menghubungi kami.

Data Program Sensitif

Informasi yang diserahkan untuk beasiswa atau bantuan sosial (termasuk kondisi keuangan atau keluarga) diperlakukan sebagai data sensitif. Digunakan hanya untuk evaluasi program, dijaga kerahasiaannya, dan tidak dibagikan di luar tim administrasi yayasan.
